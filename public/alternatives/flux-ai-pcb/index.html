Flux.ai alternatives for PCB design

This page is about Flux.ai for circuit boards, not the similarly named AI image model. The right alternative depends on which part of electronics design is slowing you down.

NeedConsiderWhy
Free, mature desktop ECADKiCadOpen-source schematic capture and PCB layout with broad professional and community use.
Physics-driven placement and routingQuilterConsumes an existing design and generates checked physical-layout candidates.
Requirements-to-schematic automationCircuit MindAutomates architecture, component selection, schematic, and BOM work.
Agent review and immutable handoffRoarCADUses bounded browser tools, preview-first changes, human-only approval, and portable checkpoints.

When RoarCAD is the relevant alternative

RoarCAD is relevant when you already value agent assistance but do not want the agent to silently alter stored state or start manufacturing. It is free and MIT-licensed, and it keeps project history in the browser. It is not the right choice when you need a mature professional editor, automated high-complexity layout, or verified signal-integrity closure.

Honest limit: switching tools does not remove the need for electrical review, DFM, prototype bring-up, and physical testing.
