Complete journey to inspect

  1. Open RoarCAD and select the fabrication-ready indicator.
  2. Create a checkpoint and open it in a clean browser profile.
  3. Continue only after downloading the local backup.
  4. Preview a component move and confirm the revision identifier is unchanged.
  5. Use the visible Approve & apply button and confirm a new revision appears.
  6. Return a checkpoint and inspect the common ancestor and semantic diff before adopting.
  7. Open PocketRoar and confirm engineering export remains distinct from fabrication readiness.

Known limits

  • No automated test proves a physical PCB will function.
  • No current evidence proves the PocketRoar candidate can ingest video on iPhone.
  • No claim of professional ECAD replacement, signal-integrity signoff, DFM approval, compliance, or production yield is made.
  • Checkpoint hashes detect modification; they do not authenticate the sender.
  • Live JLCPCB quoting remains disabled without an approved account-specific API contract.
Audit trail: inspect the CI history, test source, and evaluation records.
Evidence is dated. Recheck source, CI, deployment, browser behavior, and physical hardware independently before making a release claim.
