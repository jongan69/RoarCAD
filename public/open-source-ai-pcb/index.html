Free and open source

A free AI-assisted PCB workflow with human approval

RoarCAD costs $0 to use, requires no account for its local workflow, and publishes its TypeScript source under the MIT license.

Included

  • structured PCB drafting and import
  • PCB and schematic views
  • four bounded WebMCP tools
  • immutable local revisions
  • portable collaboration checkpoints
  • engineering and fabrication gates

Not included

  • verified teammate identity
  • private cloud workspaces
  • real-time coediting
  • automatic divergent-board merges
  • fabrication certification
  • proof that a physical design works

How local-first works

Projects are stored in the browser with IndexedDB. A checkpoint packages one immutable head revision and its ancestry into a compressed URL fragment or JSON file. The recipient reviews it before continuing or adopting it. Possession of a checkpoint grants read access, and its hash proves unchanged bytes—not the sender’s identity.

Why it is free

The contest version uses browser storage and native web capabilities instead of accounts, a hosted database, or real-time infrastructure. That keeps the core design, review, and handoff workflow auditable and inexpensive. Manufacturing still costs money through the chosen fabricator, and RoarCAD never invents a quote.
